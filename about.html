Who We Are

Welcome to ThereDeYe.in (The Red Eye) – India's growing educational cybersecurity awareness platform. Our mission is simple: Make Internet Safe for Everyone.

In today's digital world, online scams, phishing links, UPI frauds, data breaches, and fake loan apps are increasing daily. Many people don't know how to check if their email is leaked or if a link is safe. We created this platform to solve that problem.

We are not a government agency. We are a team of cyber awareness enthusiasts who believe that education is the best defense against cybercrime. Our website provides 43+ free educational tools for learning purposes only.

What We Do - Our 43 Educational Tools

  • Email Breach Check: Uses XposedOrNot API to teach users about data leaks.
  • Phone & UPI Validation: Format-based educational validation, no data stored.
  • Link Scanner & WHOIS: Uses VirusTotal and WHOIS public data.
  • IP, DNS, SSL, Device Info: Uses ipapi.co, Google DNS, SSLLabs.
  • Awareness Tools: Info about 1930, cybercrime.gov.in, PIB Fact Check, RBI list.
  • Game Account Appeal Generator: ONLY letter FORMAT for education. We do NOT hack/unban. Contact official game support.
