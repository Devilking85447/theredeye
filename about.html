Global To World (GTW) was started with one simple idea - In today's world, people feel lonely even after having 1000s of followers online. We wanted to create a place where real people can meet real friends, safely.

We are from Himachal Pradesh, India. We started GTW for people from small towns like Solan, Shimla, Mandi to big cities like Delhi, Mumbai - everyone deserves a real friend.
