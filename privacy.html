Privacy Policy - Global To World

Last Updated: November 19, 2026

Welcome to Global To World (GTW). Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

1. What We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your Name, Email, Age, City, Gender and Bio which you provide. We do not collect your password in plain text. Passwords are securely handled by Firebase Authentication.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used only to show your profile to other verified users, to allow you to login safely, and to improve GTW. We never sell your personal data to anyone.

3. Photos

Profile photos you upload are visible to other users. Please do not upload private, nude or other people's photos. Such accounts will be banned permanently.

4. Cookies & Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Google may use cookies to show relevant ads based on your interests. You can control ad personalization from Google Ad Settings. We do not control Google's cookies.

5. Data Security

Your data is stored securely on Firebase (Google). We use Email Verification to prevent fake accounts. Never share your OTP, Password or Bank Details with anyone, even if someone claims to be from GTW team.

6. Your Rights

You can edit your profile anytime. If you want to delete your account and data, contact us on our Contact page. We will delete your account within 7 working days.

7. 18+ Only

GTW is strictly for users 18 years and above. If we find a user below 18, the account will be deleted immediately.

8. Contact Us

If you have any question about this Privacy Policy, visit our Contact Page.
