1. Top 10 Places To Visit In Himachal Pradesh in 2026

Himachal Pradesh is known for its beautiful mountains, snowfall, and friendly people. Shimla, Manali, Kufri, Solan, Mandi, Dharamshala, Kasol, Spiti Valley, Dalhousie and Chamba. Best time to visit is March to June and December to February for snowfall.

2. Kufri Travel Guide - Snow, Horse Ride & Best Time

Kufri is just 20 km from Shimla and is famous for snow and adventure. Budget for one day trip is around Rs 1000 to Rs 1500 per person.

3. How To Make Real Friends Online Safely?

Never share OTP, password, bank details. Always talk respectfully, meet in public places, and inform your family.

4. Solan - The Mushroom City of India

Solan is known as Mushroom City. Places to visit nearby are Kasauli, Chail, Jatoli Shiv Temple, and Shoolini Temple.

5. 10 Best Budget Trips in India Under Rs 5000

Kasol, Rishikesh, McLeodganj, Jaipur, Udaipur, Manali, Dharamshala, Amritsar, Pushkar and Gokarna.

6. Himachali Culture, Food & Festivals

Himachal is famous for Nati dance, woolen caps, Kullu Dussehra and Mandi Shivratri. Traditional dishes are Dham, Siddu, Tudkiya Bhath.
