About Us

The Red Eye Village is a free worldwide chat village started from Kumharsain, Himachal Pradesh.

Our mission is simple: Puri duniya ek gaon ki tarah baat kar sake - bina paise, bina number, sirf email se. Yaha India, USA, UK, Dubai - sab log ek sath baat kar sakte hain.

We believe in free speech, respect, and real connection. No ads, no tracking, just pure baat-cheet.
