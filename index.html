Our Free Online Tools - Complete List and Features

At The Red Eye Village, we provide 12 powerful, free, and private online tools that are useful in daily life. All tools work directly in your browser without any login, registration, or data storage. Below is the complete list of our tools with detailed description.

1. QR Code Generator: Our QR Code Generator is a fast and secure tool that allows you to create a QR code for any type of information. You can enter plain text, website URL, email address, phone number, WiFi password, or UPI payment ID like yourname@oksbi or yourname@paytm. The tool instantly generates a high-quality QR code image. You can download the QR code in PNG format and print it for your shop, business card, product packaging, restaurant menu, or for receiving UPI payments. This tool is extremely popular among small business owners, shopkeepers, and street vendors in India who want to accept digital payments through Google Pay, PhonePe, and Paytm.

2. Strong Password Generator: Online security is very important. Many people use weak passwords like 123456 or their name, which can be easily hacked. Our Strong Password Generator creates a highly secure, random 16-character password that includes uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special symbols. This password is almost impossible to guess or hack through brute force attack. You can use it to secure your Facebook, Instagram, Gmail, and banking accounts. The password is generated locally in your browser and we do not store it.

3. Word Counter Tool: The Word Counter is essential for students, teachers, bloggers, and professionals. Paste or type your text and the tool will instantly count words and characters. Many assignments have a word limit of 500, 1000, or 1500 words. This tool helps you stay within the limit and track your writing progress in real time.

4. Age Calculator: Our Age Calculator is accurate and popular. Select your date of birth and it will calculate your exact age in years, months, and days. It also shows total days lived and next birthday. It is useful for government forms, school admission, competitive exams like SSC, UPSC, Police Recruitment, passport applications, and job applications where exact age is required.

5. BMI Calculator: The BMI Calculator is a health tool that checks if your body weight is healthy according to your height. Enter height in centimeters and weight in kilograms. The tool calculates Body Mass Index using BMI = weight divided by height squared. It shows category as per WHO standards - Underweight, Normal, Overweight, or Obese.

6. EMI Calculator: The EMI Calculator is a financial planning tool that calculates your monthly loan installment before taking a loan. Enter Loan Amount, Annual Interest Rate, and Tenure in Months. The tool uses standard EMI formula to calculate monthly EMI, total interest, and total payable amount. Useful for home loan, car loan, bike loan, education loan, or personal loan from banks like SBI, HDFC, ICICI.

7. Case Converter: The Case Converter is a text formatting tool. Paste any paragraph and convert it to UPPERCASE, lowercase, or Title Case with one click. It saves time for documentation and data entry work.

8. Image Compressor: The Image Compressor reduces file size of images without losing much visible quality. Government job portals have strict limits of 50KB, 100KB, or 200KB for photos and signatures. Our tool compresses images directly in your browser, so private photos are never uploaded to any server.

9. Image to PDF Converter: This tool converts JPG, JPEG, or PNG images into a single PDF file. Useful for students who need to convert handwritten notes, scanned certificates, or forms into PDF format for online submission.

10. Text to Speech: The Text to Speech reads any English text aloud. It is helpful for visually impaired people and students who want to listen to study notes while doing other work.

11. YouTube Thumbnail Downloader: This tool is for YouTubers, video editors, and designers. Paste any YouTube video link and the tool will extract the highest quality HD thumbnail image in maxresdefault quality. You can download it for reference and inspiration.

12. World Village Talk (WVT): WVT is our main community project. It is an anonymous global chat platform where you can talk to strangers from around the world without sharing your phone number, email, or Instagram ID. Just like people gather at the village square in Himachal, WVT is an online village square for the whole world.

About Us - The Red Eye Village Story

The Red Eye Village (theredeye.co.in) - A Digital Village From The Himalayas: The Red Eye Village is not just a website, it is a vision, a movement, and a digital community started from a small hill town called Kumharsain in Shimla district, Himachal Pradesh, India, by its founder Praveen Bansal in early 2026. The idea was born from a simple observation - in a real village in Himachal, people live like a family. They help each other without asking for money, they share tools, they sit together at the village center, known locally as Chaupal, in the evening to talk about life, farming, family, and the world. The founder wanted to bring that same village culture to the internet.

Why The Name Red Eye Village: The name has deep meaning. Red Eye symbolizes someone who is awake, watching, working hard late at night for his dreams. The founder spent many nights with red eyes, coding, learning, and building this platform after his daily work. It also symbolizes focus and passion. Village represents community, trust, and togetherness. So The Red Eye Village means a community of hardworking, passionate people who are awake for their dreams and help each other like a real village.

Our Journey From 2 Tools to 12 Tools: When we started in January 2026, we had only two simple tools - a QR Code Generator and a Password Generator. We made them because we ourselves needed them and other websites were full of spam ads and asked for logins. We wanted something simple, fast, and private. We shared the site with friends in Kumharsain, and they loved it. They said please add more tools like Age Calculator, BMI Calculator. So we listened to our users. In February 2026, we added Word Counter, Age Calculator, BMI Calculator, and EMI Calculator. In March 2026, we added Case Converter, Image Compressor, Image to PDF, Text to Speech, and YouTube Thumbnail Downloader. Each tool was added based on real user demand from students, shopkeepers, teachers, and YouTubers across India.

What is World Village Talk: WVT is our most ambitious project. It is a free, anonymous global chat platform. In today's world, to talk to someone, you need their phone number, Instagram ID, or you need to pay for apps. Many people feel lonely but are scared to share their private number. WVT solves this. It is like an online Chaupal of our village, but for the entire world. You can come, select a topic room like Student Life, Love and Friendship, Himachal Culture, Global Chat, and start talking to strangers without revealing your phone number or real name. You can make friends from Delhi, Mumbai, Nepal, USA, and learn about different cultures. Our moderation system ensures chat remains respectful. Our mission is to connect 100,000 users by end of 2027.

Our Core Values Privacy Free Simple: Our first value is Privacy. All our tools run directly in your browser using JavaScript. This means when you generate a QR code for your UPI payment, or generate a password for your bank account, or compress your private photo for a government job form, that data never goes to our server. It stays on your phone or laptop. We cannot see it, we cannot store it, we cannot sell it. This is why our tools are trusted by thousands of users. Our second value is Free Forever. We believe essential digital tools should be free. A student from a poor family should not have to pay to count words for his assignment or to convert an image to PDF for a scholarship form. All 12 tools will always be free. Our third value is Simplicity. No forced login, no email verification, no app download. Just open theredeye.co.in, use the tool, and leave. It works on even a slow 2G internet connection in the hills.

Who Uses Us and Future Vision: Our users are diverse. Students use Word Counter and Image to PDF for college assignments. Shopkeepers and small business owners from Himachal, Punjab, UP, Bihar use QR Generator to accept UPI payments. Young professionals use Password Generator to keep their accounts safe from hackers. Families use Age Calculator to check exact age for government jobs like UPSC, SSC, Police Bharti, and BMI Calculator to track health. People planning to buy a home or bike use EMI Calculator to compare loan offers from SBI, HDFC, ICICI banks. YouTubers and video editors use YT Thumbnail Downloader for inspiration. And lonely hearts, students away from home, and curious minds use WVT to talk to new people and feel connected. In 2027, we plan to add 20 more tools including Resume Builder, Invoice Generator, AI Photo Enhancer, Background Remover, and Voice to Text in Hindi. Privacy Policy - 100% Private and Secure

Introduction and Commitment to Privacy: At The Red Eye Village, operated by Praveen Bansal from Kumharsain, Himachal Pradesh, your privacy is our top priority and foundation of our platform. This detailed Privacy Policy explains in simple English how we handle your information when you use our 12 free online tools and World Village Talk platform. We have designed our entire system with privacy first approach. Unlike many other websites that collect your personal data, track you, and sell your data to advertisers, we do the opposite. We collect minimum data and we never sell your data.

1. No Data Collection for All 12 Tools - Client Side Processing: This is the most important point. All our 12 tools work completely on the client side, meaning directly inside your web browser on your device using JavaScript technology. When you enter text to generate a QR code, when you generate a strong password, when you type an essay in Word Counter, when you upload an image to compress it, that data is processed locally in your browser's memory and is never sent to our web server. We do not have access to it. We do not store it in any database. We do not log it. After you close the browser tab, the data is automatically cleared from your device's memory. This makes our tools 100 percent private and more secure than even many paid tools that upload your files to their servers. For example, when a student uploads her private photo for compressing to 50KB for a government job form, that photo never leaves her phone. It is compressed in her phone itself. This is our guarantee.

2. What Information We Do Collect - Minimal and Anonymous: We believe in collecting as little as possible. We do not ask for your name, phone number, email address, Aadhaar number, or any personal identification to use our tools. You can use all tools without creating an account or logging in. The only information we may collect automatically is non-personal, anonymous technical information that all websites collect, such as your browser type, device type, approximate location based on IP address at city level, and which pages you visited on our site. This is collected through standard server logs and Google Analytics to understand which tools are popular and to improve our website speed. This anonymous data cannot be used to identify you personally.

For example, if you searched for shoes on Google, you may see shoe ads on our site. These cookies are set and controlled by Google, not by us. You have full control over cookies. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time. You can also opt-out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. We do not have access to Google's cookies or the data they collect. We do not control which ads Google shows, but we have blocked sensitive categories like adult content, gambling, and misleading ads.

4. World Village Talk Chat Privacy: WVT is our anonymous global chat platform. We designed it to be as private as possible. You do not need to provide your real name, phone number, email, or photo to join WVT. You can join with a random nickname. Chat messages in WVT are temporary.
